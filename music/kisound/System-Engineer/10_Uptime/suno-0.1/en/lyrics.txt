Shutdown. The fans go quiet, then the silence falls.

fsck runs through it all, an hour long, before it lets me in.
Login. The server's alive. But the service isn't there,
someone started it by hand back then, never enabled anywhere.
systemctl enable, systemctl start, and now I know its role:
it hands out the licenses for the CAD software. I breathe. It's whole.
