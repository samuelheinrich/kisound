THEMA: DNS-Probleme, Network Engineer Humor, Fehlersuche

LYRIC PROMPT:
Verse:
They call me up, the network is down,
everybody staring at the blinking crown.
Ping is clean and the routes are fine,
but the hostname vanished from the line.

Pre-Chorus:
Check the cable, check the port,
check the firewall report.
Trace complete, no mystery,
then I ask: what about DNS?

Chorus:
It's always DNS,
not the switch, not the mess.
It's always DNS,
why do we always guess?

Verse 2:
Cache is old, TTL too high,
split brain zones make admins cry.
Forward lookup, reverse pain,
CNAME dancing in the rain.

Bridge:
Flush it.
Query it.
Dig it.
Fix it.
Not the network.
It's DNS.

Final Chorus:
It's always DNS,
from the helpdesk to the stress.
It's always DNS,
close the case, confess.

STYLE PROMPT:
Bouncy Tech-House / Electro-Techno mit hartem Bassfundament. Tempo ca. 124-128 BPM. Humorvoller, einpraegsamer Vocal-Hook, aber Produktion bleibt fett und modern. Abschnittsnamen nicht singen. Knackige Kick, rollende Bassline, kurze digitale Blips als DNS-Query-Sounds. Im Break Call-and-response mit "It's always DNS". Leicht funky Groove, gute Club-Energie, weniger duester als die anderen Tracks, aber weiterhin elektronisch, basslastig und technisch.
